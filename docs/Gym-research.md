## Status and source conventions

- `VERIFIED_OFFICIAL`: the report describes the evidence as official/verified (not independently rechecked for this normalization). A report's claim of verification is not equivalent to a fresh verification in this task.
- `PUBLICLY_REPORTED`: attributed to a public directory, aggregator, partner listing, or source report.
- `INFERRED`: interpretation or synthesis rather than a directly stated business fact.
- `NOT_FOUND`: the report says it was not found or does not supply it.
- `CONFLICTING_SOURCES`: source report explicitly records materially different values.
- Sources use the source IDs and URLs in `SOURCE_LOG`. The PDF does not provide a research/access date, so none is invented.
